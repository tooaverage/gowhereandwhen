The year at a glance

Germany is a classic four-season destination, and Berlin sets the ratings below. From May to September the weather is mild and the festival calendar is full, with daylight stretching toward 10pm at midsummer. The shoulder months of May, June and September are the most comfortable.

Two seasonal events drive a lot of trips: Oktoberfest, which despite the name runs from mid September into early October in Munich, and the Christmas markets, which open across the country in late November and run through December. Winter is otherwise cold and short on light.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Berlin and the north

Cool and flat, best from late spring to early autumn. The north coast is breezy and made for summer.

02

Bavaria and Munich

Home of Oktoberfest in late September and a gateway to the Alps. Summer for hiking, winter for the markets and snow.

03

The Alps and the south

Warm valleys and cool peaks in summer, ski resorts from December to March in the Bavarian Alps.

The classic Germany loop

Berlin south through Dresden and Nuremberg to Munich, then west to Cologne and up to Hamburg. Every stop is on the rail network, and a Deutschland-Ticket or rail pass covers most of it.

Lines show the order of stops, not the roads or ferry tracks.

  1. Berlin

    Museums, the Wall, the nightlife. Most loops start here.

  2. Leipzig

    Bach, the peaceful revolution and a cheap, lively bar scene.

  3. Dresden

    Baroque old town on the Elbe, with Saxon Switzerland a day trip away.

  4. Nuremberg

    Medieval walls, the castle and the best bratwurst in the country.

  5. Munich

    Beer halls and the English Garden. Day trip to Neuschwanstein castle and the Alps.

  6. Garmisch

    The Zugspitze, alpine trails and the Partnach gorge.

  7. Freiburg

    Gateway to the Black Forest, with the sunniest weather in the country.

  8. Heidelberg

    A castle above the Neckar and the old university town below it.

  9. Bacharach

    Castles and vineyards along the Rhine gorge. Take the river boat one way.

  10. Cologne

    The cathedral, the Rhine and the Kölsch bars. Add a Rhine castle stop on the way.

  11. Hamburg

    The harbour, Speicherstadt and the Reeperbahn. Two hours back to Berlin to fly out.

Best time to visit Germany for

Cityscapes and culture

May, June and September, with mild weather, long days and lighter crowds than high summer.

Oktoberfest

Mid September to early October in Munich. Book accommodation across Bavaria two to three months ahead.

Christmas markets

Late November through December, when squares from Nuremberg to Cologne fill with stalls and mulled wine.

Hiking and the Alps

July and August for the warmest mountain weather and open high trails in the south.

What is on, and when

Oktoberfest, late September

Munich fills its beer tents for the world's biggest folk festival, running into early October.

Christmas markets, December

City squares across the country become glowing markets from late November.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Germany?
May to September for the mildest weather and the fullest calendar, with May, June and September the most comfortable and least crowded.
When is Oktoberfest?
Despite the name it runs from mid September into the first weekend of October, in Munich. Accommodation books out across Bavaria, so plan early.
When do the Christmas markets open?
Most open in the last week of November and run through December, closing around Christmas.
