Where you go changes when you go

01

Lisbon

Warm summer days and wetter late autumn. Spring and early autumn suit city walks.

02

Faro and the Algarve

Faro has little summer rain. Sea conditions need a separate check.

03

Porto and the north

Porto has more autumn and winter rain than Lisbon. Inland Douro weather differs.

04

Évora and Alentejo

Inland summer days get hot. Spring and autumn are easier for outdoor walks.

Best time to visit Portugal for

City walks

April to June or September for Lisbon and Porto. Check rain in Porto.

Algarve coast

June to September is dry in Faro. Check sea and beach conditions separately.

Alentejo towns

March to May or October avoids Évora’s hottest average days.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Portugal?
For mainland city walks, try April to June or September. Pick a region before using a country-wide month.
When is the best time for the Algarve?
Faro is driest from June to September. Weather alone cannot rate sea conditions or crowds.
Is Portugal warm in winter?
Faro has milder winter days than Porto, but both get rain. Island weather differs.
