The year at a glance

French Polynesia scatters 118 islands across an area the size of Europe: Tahiti's green peaks, Bora Bora's lagoon and overwater bungalows, and the diving atolls of the Tuamotus. It is warm year round, with a drier southern-winter half and a hot, wet summer. We rate on Papeete, Tahiti's capital.

May to October is the season to aim for: rainfall drops to a third of the summer's, humidity eases and the trade winds blow. It is also peak season, so the famous resorts book out early. November to April is greener and cheaper, with rain in heavy bursts and only a rare brush with cyclones.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Tahiti and Moorea

The gateway islands: peaks, black-sand beaches and ferry-close Moorea. Driest May to October, with the Heiva festival in July.

02

Bora Bora and the Society Islands

The overwater-bungalow lagoons of Bora Bora, Taha'a and Huahine, at their sunniest in the dry season.

03

The Tuamotus

Rangiroa and Fakarava, world-class pass diving on drier, lower atolls that shrug off much of Tahiti's rain.

Best time to visit French Polynesia for

Overwater-bungalow trips

May to October for the surest sun. Book far ahead for July and August.

Diving and snorkelling

Year round in 26 to 29 degree water; the dry season has the calmest seas, and humpbacks pass August to October.

Surfing

The big southern swells hit Teahupo'o May to October; gentler reef breaks run all year.

Best value

November to April, when resort rates ease and the islands turn green, accepting bursts of heavy rain.

What is on, and when

A month of dance, drumming and outrigger racing, the biggest festival in the South Pacific.

Whale season, Aug to Oct

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Bora Bora and Tahiti?
May to October, the dry season, with July and August the driest months. September and October keep the weather with slightly thinner crowds.
Does French Polynesia get cyclones?
Rarely. The islands sit east of the main South Pacific cyclone belt, with only occasional storms December to March in unusual years.
Is the wet season worth it for the lower prices?
Often, yes. Rain tends to fall in short, heavy bursts with sun between, and overwater bungalow rates drop meaningfully. December is the wettest gamble.
