When to go to Kiribati

Go from roughly June to October, the drier trade-wind months on Tarawa. Kiribati sits on the equator, so there is no cyclone season at all, but rainfall swings hard between El Nino and La Nina years, so treat every average loosely.

In short

Kiribati in a nutshell

Kiribati is ocean more than land: 33 atolls straddling both the equator and the date line, spread across an area wider than the continental United States. Tarawa, the capital atoll, carries the country's WWII history; far-flung Kiritimati (Christmas Island) is a world-class bonefishing flat. We rate on Tarawa.

The year splits gently rather than sharply: December to April is the wetter half, June to October the drier, breezier one. The equator spares Kiribati cyclones entirely, and the heat is remarkably constant, 31 degrees nearly every day. The big variable is El Nino, which can double the rain in a wet year; La Nina years can run bone dry.

Go for

Best time to visit Kiribati for

Bonefishing

Year round on Kiritimati's flats, one of the sport's great destinations, with calm dawn tides the daily rhythm.

WWII history

Tarawa's battle sites and relics, easiest to walk in the drier mid-year months.

Snorkelling the lagoons

June to October, when the trades steady the water. It is warm, around 29 degrees, every month.

The date line

Kiribati is the first country to see each new day, and greeted the millennium first at aptly renamed Millennium Island.

Questions

Kiribati travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Kiribati?
Roughly June to October, the drier trade-wind months on Tarawa. Rain varies hugely with El Nino and La Nina, so any month can surprise.
Does Kiribati get cyclones?
No. The islands sit on the equator, outside cyclone latitudes, one genuine worry crossed off a Pacific trip.
How do you get to Kiribati?
Fiji Airways flies Nadi to Tarawa, and Nauru Airlines connects via Brisbane and Nauru. Kiritimati has its own separate service from Honolulu and Fiji.
