The year at a glance

Vanuatu is an 80-island chain of active volcanoes, blue holes, kastom villages and reef: the South Pacific at its rawest. It is warm year round and splits into a mild dry season and a hot, humid wet one. We rate on Port Vila, the capital on Efate.

May to October is the time: drier, breezier, with August the driest month and cool, comfortable nights. November to April brings heavy rain, sticky heat and real cyclone risk, at its highest January to March. Island flights and boat trips all run more reliably in the dry half.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Efate and Port Vila

The capital, harbour resorts, and the round-island road: blue lagoons, waterfalls and beaches. Best May to October.

02

Tanna and Mount Yasur

The world's most accessible active volcano, glowing at the rim after dark. Dry-season evenings give the clearest views.

03

Espiritu Santo

Champagne Beach, the blue holes and the SS President Coolidge, one of the world's great wreck dives.

Best time to visit Vanuatu for

The volcano

May to October, when dry trails and clearer skies make the Yasur rim at dusk most reliable.

Diving and snorkelling

The dry season for visibility on the Coolidge and Santo's reefs, in warm water year round.

Kastom culture

April to June for Pentecost Island's land diving, the original bungee, on towers of vine and wood.

Best value

November to April, accepting heat, rain and cyclone-season flexibility for quiet resorts and green islands.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Vanuatu?
May to October, the dry season. August is the driest month, and April to June adds the Pentecost land diving.
When is cyclone season in Vanuatu?
November to April, and Vanuatu takes direct hits more often than most of the Pacific. Book that half of the year with flexibility and insurance.
Can you always visit Mount Yasur?
The volcano is active and access depends on its alert level, checked daily. Trips run year round but dry-season conditions are the most reliable.
