The year at a glance

The Philippines is over seven thousand islands, hot and tropical, with a clear dry and wet season. We rate on Manila, where the dry months from December to May are the time for the beaches, diving and island hopping.

The wet season from June to November brings heavy rain and the bulk of the typhoon risk, which is highest from July to October. Some regions, like parts of Mindanao and the eastern seaboard, run on a different rainfall pattern, but for most travellers the dry-season rule holds.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Palawan and the Visayas

Boracay, El Nido and Cebu are at their best in the dry season, December to May, with calm, clear water.

02

Manila and Luzon

Hot and humid, driest and most comfortable from December to April, with the rains arriving by June.

03

Diving destinations

The dry months bring the calmest seas and best visibility for Tubbataha, Apo Reef and the wreck-diving in Coron.

Manila, Palawan and the Visayas

A night in Manila, a flight to Palawan for El Nido and Coron, then Cebu for Moalboal, a ferry to Bohol and a flight to Siargao to surf. Go in the dry season, December to April. Siargao surfs best from September to November.

Lines show the order of stops, not the roads or ferry tracks.

  1. Manila

    Intramuros, then a bed near the airport for the early flight out.

  2. Banaue

    The rice terraces at Batad and Bangaan, walked in a day with a guide.

  3. Bus back, then fly to Palawan

  4. El Nido

    Island hopping tours A to D through lagoons and limestone cliffs. Nacpan beach for a day off.

  5. Coron

    Kayangan lake, the Japanese wrecks for divers, and hot springs at dusk.

  6. Boracay

    White Beach, sunset paraws and the party. Skip it if that is not your thing.

  7. Moalboal

    The sardine run right off the beach, and Kawasan falls canyoneering.

  8. Bohol

    Chocolate Hills, tarsiers and the Loboc river, from a base on Panglao.

  9. Siquijor

    Waterfalls, cliff jumps and empty roads. The quietest island on the route.

  10. Siargao

    Cloud 9 surf, island hopping to Naked and Daku, and Sugba lagoon. Fly home via Cebu or Manila.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit the Philippines?
The dry season, December to May, with sunny weather and calm seas. January to April are the driest and most reliable for the islands.
When is typhoon season in the Philippines?
Roughly June to November, with the highest risk from July to October. Storms mostly affect the north and east of the country.
Is the wet season a bad time to visit?
It brings heavier rain and storm risk, but prices fall and some southern regions stay drier. Travel insurance and a flexible plan help.
