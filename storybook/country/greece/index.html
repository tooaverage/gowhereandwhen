The year at a glance

Greece runs on a long, dry Mediterranean summer. The month ratings track Athens, where late spring and early autumn bring warm, rainless days, blooming or golden landscapes, and a sea that stays swimmable from late May into October. The peak of July and August is hot and very busy.

The islands largely follow the same rhythm but with a catch: outside the May to October season, ferries thin out and many hotels and tavernas close, especially on the smaller islands. The mainland and Athens stay open and pleasant for sightseeing year round.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Athens and the mainland

Hot summers and mild winters. Spring and autumn are ideal for the Acropolis and ancient sites, without the summer heat.

02

The Cyclades and islands

Santorini, Mykonos and the rest are at their best from May to mid June and late August into September, warm but a touch calmer.

03

Crete and the south

The warmest, longest season of all, with swimming well into October and mild winters.

Athens, Meteora and down the Cyclades to Crete

A few days in Athens, a train north to the monasteries at Meteora, then ferries south through the Cyclades to Santorini and on to Crete. Late May, June and September are the sweet spot: ferries running, sea warm, crowds thin.

Lines show the order of stops, not the roads or ferry tracks.

  1. Athens

    The Acropolis, Plaka and Psyrri. Base for the train north and the ferries south.

  2. Nafplio

    A Venetian harbour town, with Epidaurus and Mycenae close by.

  3. Meteora

    Monasteries on rock pillars. One full day covers the main ones; cover shoulders and knees.

  4. Train back, then the ferry from Piraeus

  5. Paros

    Naoussa harbour, Parikia and the beaches in between. A sociable hub island.

  6. Naxos

    The biggest Cycladic island: long beaches, hill villages, and the best value of the group.

  7. Milos

    Volcanic coves and the moonscape at Sarakiniko. Rent a scooter.

  8. Santorini

    The caldera, Oia at sunset and the Fira to Oia walk. Two nights is plenty.

  9. Heraklion, Crete

    Knossos, the old harbour, then Chania or the Samaria gorge. Fly home from Heraklion.

  10. Chania

    The prettiest harbour in Crete, and the base for the Samaria gorge and Balos.

Best time to visit Greece for

Islands and beaches

Late May to mid June and September, with warm seas, sunshine and fewer crowds than the August rush.

Ancient sites

April, May, June, September and October, when Athens and Delphi are pleasant rather than scorching.

Nightlife

July and August, when Mykonos and the party islands are at full tilt.

Quiet and value

May and October bring the best mix of good weather, open tavernas and cheaper rates.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Greece?
May to June and September to October, with warm, dry weather, swimmable seas and far smaller crowds than July and August.
Can you visit the Greek islands in winter?
You can, but many hotels and tavernas close and ferries run less often, especially on the smaller islands. Crete and the larger islands stay more active.
When is the sea warmest in Greece?
September, after a full summer of sun, which is why early autumn is so popular for swimming.
