The year at a glance

Sri Lanka is small but has two monsoons, so somewhere on the island is usually in season. These ratings follow Colombo on the west coast, where the prime, driest window runs from December to March, taking in the southern beaches and the cultural triangle.

When the southwest monsoon brings rain to the west and south from May, the east coast flips into its dry, sunny season, perfect for Trincomalee and the surf at Arugam Bay. The hill country around Kandy and Ella is cool and pleasant much of the year.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Colombo, Galle and the south

The main tourist coast, at its best from December to March. The southwest monsoon brings rain from May to September.

02

Trincomalee and the east

Runs opposite to the west, with its dry, sunny season from May to September.

03

Kandy and the hill country

Cool and green much of the year, with tea estates and the train to Ella loveliest in the drier months.

Best time to visit Sri Lanka for

South coast beaches

December to March for the sands around Galle, Mirissa and Unawatuna at their sunniest.

The east coast and surf

May to September for Trincomalee, Pasikuda and the famous waves at Arugam Bay.

Wildlife safaris

The drier months at each park, with Yala best from February to July and superb leopard spotting.

Whale watching

December to April off Mirissa in the south, when blue whales pass close to shore.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Sri Lanka?
December to March for the west and south coasts and the cultural sites, or May to September for the east coast. The two monsoons mean somewhere is always in season.
When is the best time for the south coast beaches?
December to March, the dry season for Galle, Mirissa and the southwest, which is also when blue whales pass offshore.
When should I visit the east coast?
May to September, when the east around Trincomalee and Arugam Bay is dry and sunny while the west is wet.
