The year at a glance

Mexico splits cleanly into a dry season and a rainy one. These ratings follow Mexico City, high in the central highlands, where the dry months from November to April bring sunny, spring-like days and the rains arrive as afternoon downpours from May. Coastal resorts run hotter and more humid.

The dry season is peak season and priced to match, with busy beaches over the December and Easter holidays. The one date to plan around on the Caribbean side is the Atlantic hurricane season, which runs June to November and is most active from August to October.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Mexico City and the highlands

Mild and spring-like year round thanks to the altitude, sunniest and driest from November to April.

02

Cancun and the Caribbean

Hot and humid, best from December to April. It sits in the hurricane belt, with the highest risk from August to October.

03

The Pacific coast

Puerto Vallarta and Oaxaca are warm and dry in winter, hot and wet in summer, with their own storm risk in late summer.

Best time to visit Mexico for

Beaches and resorts

December to April on both coasts, with warm, dry, sunny weather, though prices peak over the winter holidays.

Ruins and culture

November to April for Mexico City, Oaxaca and the Yucatan, with comfortable temperatures for exploring.

Day of the Dead

Late October into early November, one of the country's most striking and atmospheric celebrations.

Off-peak

June offers a window of cheaper travel before the worst of the storm season, with greener landscapes.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Mexico?
The dry season, November to April, with sunny, comfortable weather. December to February are the coolest and most pleasant, though prices peak over the holidays.
When is hurricane season in Mexico?
The Atlantic hurricane season runs June to November and is most active from August to October, mainly affecting the Caribbean coast around Cancun.
Is the rainy season a bad time to visit?
From May to October the rain tends to arrive as heavy afternoon storms that pass quickly, prices are lower and the landscapes green, though the coast carries storm risk.
