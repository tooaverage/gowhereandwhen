When to visit Austria for your kind of trip

The Austrian National Tourist Office recommends spring and autumn for city breaks, summer for lakes and hiking, and winter for skiing. Use the season that suits your activity, then check conditions for the place you want to visit.

What is the best month to ski in Austria?

There is no single month that wins for every resort. Austria Tourism identifies late December to early March as the core season and notes that opening periods vary. Pick a resort and compare snow conditions, lift operations and school holidays before choosing dates. A low winter score in our Vienna chart means cold weather for general sightseeing; it does not mean a bad ski holiday.
