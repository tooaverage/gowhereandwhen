The year at a glance

Austria has two clear peaks, and the month ratings track Vienna. Summer brings warm, long days for the imperial cities, the lakes of the Salzkammergut and hiking in the Alps, while winter turns the mountains into one of Europe's great ski regions.

Spring and autumn are quieter and milder, good for the cities and the wine country without the crowds, though the high mountains can be caught between seasons. Vienna and Salzburg sparkle in December for the Christmas markets.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Vienna and Salzburg

Lovely in late spring and summer, at their best in December for the Christmas markets. Winters are cold.

02

Tyrol and the Alps

Summer for hiking and the lakes, December to March for the big ski resorts of Innsbruck and St Anton.

03

The Wachau and wine country

Beautiful in late spring and at the autumn harvest, with terraced vineyards above the Danube.

Best time to visit Austria for

Cities and culture

May to September for Vienna and Salzburg, with warm days and a full calendar of music and festivals.

Skiing

December to March in Tyrol and the western Alps, with the most reliable snow at altitude.

Hiking and lakes

June to September for the Salzkammergut lakes and the Alpine trails in full flower.

Christmas markets

Late November through December, when Vienna, Salzburg and Innsbruck glow with stalls and mulled wine.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Austria?
Summer, June to September, for hiking, lakes and cities, or winter, December to March, for skiing and Christmas markets.
When is the best time for skiing in Austria?
December to March across the Tyrol and western Alps, with the deepest, most reliable snow at the higher resorts.
When are the Christmas markets in Vienna?
Mid November through December, with most markets closing around Christmas Eve.
