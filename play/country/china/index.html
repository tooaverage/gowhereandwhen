Good to know

What is the best time to visit China?
It depends on your city. Compare April and October for Beijing, then check regional weather in the table.
When are China's 2026 national travel breaks?
Labour Day runs May 1–5, and National Day runs October 1–7.
Is China too hot in summer?
Beijing, Xi'an and Guangzhou have hot July days. Kunming is cooler, while Harbin has a milder summer.
