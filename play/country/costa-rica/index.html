The year at a glance

Costa Rica keeps it simple with a dry season and a green one. San Jose, in the central highlands, anchors the ratings, where the dry months from December to April bring sunshine and the easiest conditions, and the rains return as afternoon storms from May.

The green season is not a washout: mornings are often clear, the rain comes later in the day, prices drop and the rainforest hums with wildlife. The one local twist is the Caribbean coast around Tortuguero, which is wetter in the middle of the year and drier around September and October.

Best time to visit Costa Rica for

Beaches and surfing

December to April on the Pacific for sunny, dry days, with the green season bringing bigger surf and fewer crowds.

Wildlife and rainforest

The green season, when the forests are dense and alive with wildlife, or the dry season for easier access and clearer trails.

Turtle nesting

July to October at Tortuguero on the Caribbean coast, when green turtles come ashore.

Low season

May, June and the green season for better rates, fewer crowds and vivid, green landscapes.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Costa Rica?
The dry season, December to April, with sunny weather and the easiest travel. The green season, May to November, is cheaper, quieter and green, with afternoon rains.
Is the green season a bad time to visit?
Mornings are often clear, storms roll in mid-afternoon and pass, prices fall and the rainforest is at its liveliest.
When is the best time for the Caribbean coast?
September and October, which are drier and calmer there even as the rest of the country sees its wettest months.
