The year at a glance

Jordan is mostly desert, so the trick is dodging the extremes. The ratings key off Amman, where spring and autumn bring warm, sunny days that are ideal for the long walks through Petra and the dunes of Wadi Rum.

Summer is searing in the desert and at the Dead Sea, the lowest point on Earth, while winter is cool by day and genuinely cold at night, with frost and occasional snow in Amman. The Red Sea at Aqaba stays warm and swimmable for much of the year.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Jordan?
Spring, March to May, and autumn, September to November, with warm, comfortable weather for Petra, Wadi Rum and the cities.
Is Jordan too hot in summer?
Petra, Wadi Rum and the Dead Sea are intensely hot in July and August. Spring and autumn are far more comfortable for sightseeing.
Is winter a bad time to visit Jordan?
Days are cool and clear and crowds thin, but desert nights are cold and Amman can see frost or snow, so pack warm layers.
