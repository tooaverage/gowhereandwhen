# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Travellers choosing a destination and month, then comparing regions, cities and routes within a country. The map is the primary working surface on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Show when to visit using existing climate normals, seasonal guidance and complete country guides. Country selection reveals an overlay; zoom reveals cities with their own weather. Guides retain monthly weather, cities, regions, routes and trip lengths, activities, events, hazards, accommodation, booking links, FAQs and related guides.

## Brand Personality

Exploratory, useful and characterful. Direct, plain language. The user supplied historical travel brochures and richly detailed maps as visual references.

## Anti-references

The first prototype pass was rejected. Avoid AI-looking photographic landscape artwork, content reduction, inset map dashboards, prominent score metrics, repeated numbered sections, uppercase small labels and generic card grids. Red is allowed for poor travel conditions, with orange, yellow, light green and green for improving conditions. Only place names may use uppercase. No em dashes.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the complete product while changing presentation.
- Keep the map full width with contextual overlays.
- Use seasonal graphs before score metrics.
- Make exploration reveal geographic detail and cities in a low-poly game world.
- Carry the brochure references through cartography and composition, not just a cream background.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep keyboard controls, visible focus, text alternatives for color-coded data, readable contrast and reduced-motion behavior. Use semantic tables and headings. These are implementation standards, not additional user-stated requirements.

## Latest direction

The Explorer map and guides should feel like a low-poly video game. Preserve the original meaning and full prose while redesigning the structure. Work mobile first. Keep old design rounds at stable addresses. No handwritten font. Keep accommodation booking prominent and preserve its existing integration.

## Current priorities

The world map is first a monthly weather heatmap. Every country with climate data uses red, orange, yellow, light green or green; terrain decoration is secondary. Explorer and Travel Bureau remain separate active directions, with links between them. Preserve the low-poly Japan header. Travel Bureau uses authentic photographs, upright condensed place names, brochure covers and printed cartography. Map gestures must zoom the map, labels must not overlap, and place names must not split inside a word.
