The year at a glance

South Korea has four sharp seasons, and Seoul sets the ratings below. Spring and autumn are the two highlights, when the weather is mild and dry and the country is at its most colourful, with blossom in April and foliage in late October.

Summer is hot and humid with a rainy season, the jangma, in July, while winter is cold and dry, cold enough for ski resorts in the mountains. The shoulder seasons are short but spectacular, so they draw the crowds and the highest prices.

Best time to visit South Korea for

Cherry blossom

Late March to mid April, sweeping north from Jeju to Seoul, lighting up parks and palace grounds.

Autumn foliage

Late October to early November, when the mountains and city parks turn red and gold.

Skiing

December to February in the Gangwon mountains, on the slopes that hosted the Winter Olympics.

Hiking

May to June and September to October, the mild, dry shoulders, for the national parks.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit South Korea?
Spring, April to June, for cherry blossom, and autumn, September to November, for clear skies and foliage. Both are mild, dry and the most popular times.
When is the rainy season in South Korea?
The jangma rainy season falls mainly in July, the wettest stretch of a hot, humid summer.
Is winter a good time to visit Korea?
Yes if you want skiing, snowy temples and winter festivals. It is cold and dry, with bright days and few crowds.
