Bohol · Dumaluan & Libaong

Bohol / Panglao

Long beach mornings. Plenty within reach.

Google Maps ↗
1

Best balanceAn easy sandy-beach routine with airport access and plenty nearby. Secure a tested room connection first.

  • Sandy swimming
  • Easy island base
  • Scooter days

Stay around Dumaluan or Libaong for a slower beach rhythm, then head to Alona when you want more restaurants and people. This is an established holiday island with quiet corners, rather than a remote escape.

The swimming
Dumaluan has a long sandy shoreline and shallow swimming water. Check the tide and conditions each day. A room advertised as ‘Panglao beachfront’ might be on a very different stretch of coast.
Working from here
Choose the accommodation before committing to a working stay. Ask for a call test from the actual room and backup power for both the router and your workspace. A Wi-Fi amenity alone is not evidence of dependable calls.
Getting around
A scooter makes the quieter beach areas easier to use. Bohol-Panglao Airport is on the island; inland Bohol outings need a separate trip.

The catchThe inexpensive room, the best swimming beach and the strongest connection may be three different locations. Check the exact address and beach access.

Published room examples / night

≈ CA$49–62

Bohol Sunside’s listed room categories, including its higher December–February rates. This is an inland example, not a beachfront quote. Food, transport and activities are extra.

Right on the water?

For the Dumaluan shoreline, compare the exact room location and beach access. Beachfront resorts can cost substantially more than an inland room.

The rest of the budget

Sunside also lists scooter hire at CA$11/day. Ask for a separate long-stay room quote rather than multiplying a nightly rate by 30.

San Juan · Maite & nearby coast

Siquijor

Slow coastal days, with a green interior.

Google Maps ↗
2

Best slower island rhythmA strong fit for coastal rides, waterfalls and value. Swimming depends on the exact shore and tide; calls need checking.

  • Slower pace
  • Waterfall outings
  • Scooter exploring

A good place to build a small routine: breakfast, a coastal ride, a swim, then sunset. San Juan puts cafés and places to stay within reach while the island’s interior offers a change of scene.

The swimming
Pick the actual swimming spot, not just an ocean view. Parts of the coast are shallow or rocky at low tide. Paliton and the San Juan coast are worth exploring, with conditions checked locally.
Working from here
Some properties offer backup power, but this is still a property-by-property decision. Republika advertises a generator and Wi-Fi; that does not establish call quality or overnight uptime.
Getting around
A scooter suits exploring beyond your accommodation. Arrivals commonly involve a ferry and a road transfer, so leave a buffer before an onward flight.

The catchA beautiful waterfront room does not guarantee a sandy swim outside your door. Ask for low-tide photos and test the connection before a long stay.

Published room examples / night

≈ CA$40–66

Republika lists a fan-cooled bamboo cabin at CA$40 and air-conditioned units at CA$66. These are property examples, not an island-wide average. Food and transport are extra.

Right on the water?

Republika describes its units as 10–20 metres from the shore with partial sea views. The lower cabin price is fan-only, not air-conditioned.

The rest of the budget

For a food reference, current Islamove listings include a fish-and-rice dish at CA$5 and a chicken salad at CA$7; delivery charges may apply.

Approximate Canadian dollars · Room costs, not a total daily budget. Check dates, taxes and inclusions before booking.

Your travel month

Eating well

Look for local rice meals and barbecue around town alongside the visitor cafés. Resort and international menus sit in a different price bracket.

Finding company

Small bars and dive operators provide casual company without requiring a nightlife-heavy stay. A regular salsa programme is not verified here.

  • Nature outingIsland interior

White Beach · Station 3 & Angol

Boracay

The famous beach, from its quieter end.

Google Maps ↗
3

Best for everyday swimmingStart at Station 3 or Angol for long beach walks and easier swimming. Less compelling for hikes and scooter adventures.

  • Long sandy beach
  • Walkable beach life
  • Quieter Station 3

Boracay can be a swimming-and-walking stay rather than a party holiday. Station 3 and Angol are the starting points for a quieter base, with busier restaurants and nightlife farther along White Beach.

The swimming
White Beach is the standout for a long sandy shoreline. Its western side is generally more sheltered during the northeast monsoon, but always check the day’s sea conditions.
Working from here
Book around a tested room connection and a quiet place to take calls. A larger hotel inventory gives you options, not a guarantee that every beachfront room has dependable Wi-Fi.
Getting around
Think beach walks and e-trikes rather than a scooter-and-mountain routine. Caticlan is the nearby mainland gateway, followed by a short boat transfer.

The catchIt is still a popular holiday island. Strong for beach access, weaker for solitude, mountain hikes and a very cheap month right on the water.

Room planning allowance / night

≈ CA$55–110

An editorial allowance for comparing simpler Station 3 stays, not a live quote or guaranteed range. A recent Surfside listing started around CA$55 before taxes; your dates and room type can cost more.

Right on the water?

Station 3 has accommodation beside the sand, but the least expensive category may face the garden. Confirm whether ‘beachfront’ describes the property or your room.

The rest of the budget

Allow for arrival transfers and island fees. Holiday dates and peak-season weekends can exceed the room allowance above.

Northern Mindanao · Mambajao & Agoho

Camiguin

Volcanic trails, waterfalls and an unhurried coast.

Google Maps ↗
4

Best for hikingMountains, waterfalls and good-value coastal stays. The tradeoff is fewer easy sandy swimming spots and unproven call setups.

  • Mountain hiking
  • Quieter island
  • Scooter exploring

A nature-led alternative to a resort strip, with a compact island to explore and mountains rising behind the coast. Start around Mambajao or Agoho for a base.

The swimming
Much of the coast has dark sand or rocky stretches. White Island is an offshore sandbar reached by boat, not a white-sand town where you can rent a room.
Working from here
Playa del Fuego advertises Wi-Fi, but backup power and call stability are not established by its room listing. Verify both before treating a good-value bungalow as a working base.
Getting around
The island lends itself to road exploring, with hikes planned separately. Check current air or ferry connections before linking it to another island.

The catchA stronger fit for hiking than for a long white-sand beach outside the door. Work reliability still needs to be proven at the property.

Published room examples / night

≈ CA$36–66

Playa del Fuego lists a fan-cooled standard room at CA$36 a fan beach bungalow at CA$44 and an air-conditioned beachfront bungalow at CA$66 for two people. Food and transport are extra.

Right on the water?

The listed CA$66 bungalow is beachfront, but the shore’s colour, entry and tide matter as much as the view.

The rest of the budget

Allow separately for scooters, guides and offshore boat trips. Ask for a long-stay price and exactly what is included.

Cebu province · Santa Fe

Bantayan

Sand, a small-town routine, and room to slow down.

Google Maps ↗
5

Best simple beach routineA relaxed alternative with sandy shores and scooter outings. Dedicated work options are less established in this guide.

  • Beach-first
  • Unhurried days
  • Scooter exploring

Santa Fe is the beach base, with a compact centre and a quieter feel than a large resort strip. Pick this for simple days around the shore rather than a packed itinerary.

The swimming
Santa Fe’s sandy coastline is the main draw. Tide and wind still matter; neither a sandbar photo nor a beachfront address guarantees deep, calm swimming all day.
Working from here
Treat reliable calls as something to establish with your host. This guide has not verified a dedicated workspace with backup internet and power in Santa Fe.
Getting around
Scooter outings fit the island better than mountain hiking. The usual route from Cebu involves a road journey to Hagnaya and a ferry to Santa Fe.

The catchA strong beach choice, but a less proven work base. It also involves more travel stages than landing directly on Panglao.

Listed resort examples / night

≈ CA$75–124

A PH.vacations listing for Kota Beach spans standard air-con rooms to beachfront suites. This is a third-party published range; confirm current rates directly. It is not the cheapest-room floor for Santa Fe.

Right on the water?

The same listing puts a beachfront cottage at CA$113. A standard room at a beachfront resort is not necessarily a room facing the sea.

The rest of the budget

Budget separately for the Cebu road transfer, ferry and island transport. Check the final sailing before planning a same-day connection.

Siargao

Surf, café life, and a community to work alongside.

Google Maps ↗
7

Best work community, with tradeoffsColiving and coworking are its strengths. With no surfing, calm swimming as a priority and a possible winter visit, other islands fit better.

  • Coliving options
  • Surf culture
  • Cafés & community

The clearest fit here for someone who wants a social work routine. General Luna is the busy hub; Libertad and Santa Fe offer quieter bases within riding distance. Santa Fe here is on Siargao, not Bantayan.

The swimming
This is a surf destination with reefs and tide-dependent swimming spots. Do not equate an oceanfront room with an easy, calm swimming beach.
Working from here
Coco Space advertises call booths, Starlink plus a backup ISP, and solar plus generator backup. Its listed pass is CA$11/day or CA$165/month. Confirm opening hours and call-booth availability for your schedule.
Getting around
A scooter can connect your room, workspace and surf spots. Staying farther from General Luna buys quiet but adds daily riding.

The catchThe work community is appealing, but calm swimming and winter sunshine are weaker reasons to choose it. November–January is a wetter eastern-coast window.

Before a longer stay

Check the room, not just the island.

For calls: ask for a recent speed test and a short video call from the room at your working hour. Check upload stability, noise, backup internet and whether the router stays powered during an outage.

For swimming: ask what the shore looks like at low tide. Sea view, waterfront and an easy sandy swimming beach are different things.

For boat days: leave slack around ferries and island outings. Monthly climate describes a pattern, not your forecast; wind and gale warnings can stop trips even without all-day rain. Check PAGASA marine advisories ↗
