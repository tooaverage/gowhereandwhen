When to go to the Solomon Islands

Go from June to September, the drier trade-wind months, for the calmest seas and the clearest water over the reefs and WWII wrecks. The wet season, November to April, is hotter, stickier and much wetter, with some cyclone risk.

In short

the Solomon Islands in a nutshell

The Solomon Islands are the Pacific before mass tourism: a thousand islands of jungle, lagoon and reef, and the ships and aircraft of the Guadalcanal campaign resting in dive depth. It is hot, humid and equatorial year round. We rate on Honiara, the capital on Guadalcanal.

June to September is the relative dry season, when monthly rain roughly halves, the southeast trades blow and seas settle for diving and island transfers. November to April is the wet season, with heavy rain, the odd cyclone in the far south, and March the wettest month in Honiara. Showers can arrive any day of the year.

Go for

Best time to visit the Solomon Islands for

Wreck and reef diving

June to September for the calmest seas and best visibility, in bathwater year round.

WWII history

The dry months make Guadalcanal's battlefield sites and memorials easiest to reach. August marks the 1942 landings.

Surfing

November to April, when north swells reach the breaks around Gizo and Santa Isabel.

Birding and jungle walks

The drier mid-year months, for trails that are merely muddy rather than flooded.

Questions

the Solomon Islands travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit the Solomon Islands?
June to September: roughly half the rain of the wet season, steadier trade winds and the calmest seas for diving and boat transfers.
Do the Solomon Islands get cyclones?
Occasionally, November to April, mostly brushing the southern islands. Honiara sees fewer direct hits than Fiji or Vanuatu.
Is malaria a risk in the Solomon Islands?
Yes, year round and higher in the wet season. Take medical advice on prophylaxis and pack repellent and long sleeves for dusk.
