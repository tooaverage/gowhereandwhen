The year at a glance

Tonga is the Pacific's last kingdom, 170 islands where humpback whales come to calve in clear, warm water. It sits far enough south that the dry season, May to October, is genuinely mild: July and August days top out around 24 degrees with nights dipping to 18. We rate on Nuku'alofa, the capital.

The wet season, November to April, is hot and humid with the year's heaviest rain around February, and it carries the cyclone risk. The whale season, July to October, lands neatly inside the best weather, which makes August and September the classic months to come.

Vava'u

The whale-swim and sailing capital: a sheltered maze of islands at its best July to October.

Ha'apai

Low, quiet atolls between the two: empty beaches, kiteboarding winds mid-year, whales in season.

Best time to visit Tonga for

Swimming with whales

July to October, with licensed operators out of Vava'u, Ha'apai and Tongatapu. Book months ahead.

Sailing and kayaking

The dry season, for steady trade winds and settled anchorages around Vava'u.

Beaches and reefs

May to October. The water is coolest in August, so whale swimmers often wear a spring suit.

Best value

November to April, outside whale season, when rates drop and the islands go quiet.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Tonga?
May to October for the dry season, and July to October if you want the humpback whales. August and September combine both at their peak.
When can you swim with whales in Tonga?
July to October, on licensed trips with strict rules on numbers and approach. Vava'u is the classic base, with Ha'apai quieter.
Does Tonga get cold?
Cool rather than cold: June to August days around 24 degrees and nights down to 18, so pack a layer for evenings. The sea is at its coolest then too.
