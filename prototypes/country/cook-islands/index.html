When to go to the Cook Islands

Go between June and October for the driest, breeziest weather, with September to November a warm, dry sweet spot before the rains. The damp season, December to March, is hotter and wetter and carries the cyclone risk.

In short

The year at a glance

The Cook Islands pair Polynesian ease with New Zealand-simple logistics: no big resorts, a circle-island bus on Rarotonga, and Aitutaki's lagoon an hour north. The islands are warm year round and split into a dry season and a damp one. We rate on Rarotonga, where nearly everyone lands.

April to November is the drier half, with June the driest month and pleasantly cool nights mid-year. September to November is a local favourite: the air warms back up while the rain holds off. December to March brings humid heat and hard showers that usually pass within hours, plus the cyclone season.

By region

Where you go changes when you go

01

Rarotonga

The green, peaked main island, ringed by one road and a lagoon. Best June to November.

02

Aitutaki

One of the Pacific's great lagoons, a short flight north and slightly warmer and drier than Rarotonga.

03

Atiu and the outer islands

Raised coral islands with caves, birdlife and almost no tourists. Go in the dry season for the flights' sake.

Go for

Best time to visit the Cook Islands for

Lagoon days and snorkelling

June to October for clarity and calm; Aitutaki day trips run year round.

Whale watching

July to October, when humpbacks pass close off Rarotonga's reef.

Hiking the Cross-Island Track

The dry months, when the red-earth trail over Rarotonga's spine is at its least slippery.

Best value

February to March, the quietest, wettest stretch, if you can live with humid heat and showers.

Questions

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Rarotonga?
June to October for the driest weather, with September to November a warm, dry shoulder that many locals rate the best of the year.
When is cyclone season in the Cook Islands?
November to April. Most years pass without a direct hit, but it is the season to book flexibly.
Do the Cook Islands get cold?
Mid-year nights dip below 20 degrees, enough for a light layer in the evening, and the lagoon is coolest August to October.
