The year at a glance

Canada is a summer country for most visitors. The month ratings track Toronto, where June to August brings warm, lively days and September stays pleasant before the cold sets in. The far north and the prairies are harsher, the Pacific coast around Vancouver is milder and wetter.

Winter is long and serious across most of the country, from November well into March, which is exactly the draw if you have come for skiing, snow and the aurora. Spring and autumn are the value seasons, with mild weather and lighter crowds.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Toronto, Montreal and the east

Warm, festival-filled summers and a brilliant autumn in late September and October. Winters are cold and snowy.

02

The Rockies and the west

July and August for hiking around Banff and Lake Louise, December to March for some of the continent's best skiing at Whistler.

03

Vancouver and the coast

Mild but wet most of the year. Summer is the dry, glorious exception, which is why it is so popular.

Best time to visit Canada for

June to early September, when the weather is warm, the patios are open and the days are long.

Autumn colour

Late September and October, especially in Quebec and Ontario, with crisp air and golden maples.

Skiing and the aurora

December to March for the Rockies and the best chance of northern lights in the north.

Best value

May and September offer mild weather and better prices than the July to August peak.

Canada, one region at a time

These windows suit general sightseeing. Mountain snow, lake ice and autumn colour vary each year.

Vancouver

June to September

Harbour walks, Stanley Park and the North Shore. Nearby mountain skiing is a separate winter trip.

Victoria

May to September

Gardens, the Inner Harbour and coastal walks. The island is generally milder than inland Canada.

Calgary

June to September

A city stop before the Rockies. July and August suit Lake Louise and high-country exploring; winter suits skiing.

Toronto

June to September

Waterfront days, the CN Tower and a trip to Niagara Falls. Autumn colour is another reason to visit in fall.

Montréal

June to September

Outdoor cafés, festivals and Old Montréal. September and October bring autumn colour to the wider region.

Québec City

June to September

Old Québec and Château Frontenac in warm weather. Winter is a different trip, with snowy streets and carnival season.

Halifax

July to September

The waterfront, fishing villages and Peggy’s Cove. Atlantic weather can change quickly.

Whitehorse

June to August

Long summer days for road trips. Mid-August to early April is northern-lights season when skies are dark and clear.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Canada?
Summer, June to August, for the warmest, liveliest weather, or September and October for autumn colour and fewer crowds. Insiders often pick the fall for its mild weather and quiet.
When is the cheapest time to visit Canada?
Late autumn and the non-holiday parts of winter, plus the spring shoulder, when crowds thin and prices drop.
Is Canada worth visiting in winter?
Yes, if you have come for it. Winters are cold but bring superb skiing in the Rockies, snowy city festivals, and a strong chance of the northern lights up north.
