Good to know

When is a good time to visit Malta?
For walks, compare April, May and October. Summer is hotter and much drier.
When is Malta wettest?
Luqa averages the most rain in November, then December and January.
Is Malta hot in August?
Luqa’s average daily high is 32°C in August. Check the forecast for your dates.
