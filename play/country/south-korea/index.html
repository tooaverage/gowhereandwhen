Good to know

What is the best time to visit South Korea?
For city walks, compare April to May and September to October. Check your destination before choosing a month.
When is the rainy season in South Korea?
It usually overlaps late June to late July. Check the local forecast before travel.
Is winter a good time to visit Korea?
Yes for winter activities. Busan is milder than Seoul; mountain snow needs a local report.
