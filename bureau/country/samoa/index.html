The year at a glance

Samoa is the heart of Polynesia: two lush volcanic islands of waterfalls, blowholes, village greens and open beach fales. It is warm year round, and the year splits cleanly into a drier, breezier half and a hot, wet half. We rate on Apia, the capital on Upolu.

May to October is the dry season, with June to September the driest, sunniest stretch. November to April brings heavy downpours, sticky heat and the cyclone risk, with the heaviest rain from December to February. The Teuila Festival, Samoa's biggest cultural event, fills Apia in early September.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Upolu

Apia, the To Sua Ocean Trench and the south-coast beaches. Best in the dry season, May to October.

02

Savai'i

The bigger, quieter island: lava fields, blowholes and village Samoa at its most traditional. Same seasons, even fewer crowds.

03

Waterfalls and the interior

Falls thunder hardest at the wet edges of the year and shrink by late dry season. The lookout roads are easiest when dry.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Samoa?
The dry season, May to October. June to September is the driest, sunniest stretch, and early September adds the Teuila Festival.
When is cyclone season in Samoa?
November to April, alongside the wettest months. Storms are occasional rather than constant, but it is the season to book with flexibility.
Samoa or American Samoa?
They share the same weather and a dateline quirk: independent Samoa is a day ahead. Ferries and short flights link Apia with Pago Pago, so the dry season suits both.
