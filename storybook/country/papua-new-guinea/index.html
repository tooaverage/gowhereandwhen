The year at a glance

Papua New Guinea is the wild edge of the Pacific: 800 languages, Highlands sing-sings, birds of paradise and some of the world's richest reefs. Weather varies hugely by region, so we rate on Port Moresby, the gateway, which has a sharply defined dry season.

June to October is the time: the capital and south coast turn genuinely dry, trails firm up, and the two great Highlands shows, Mount Hagen in August and Goroka in September, land back to back. December to April is wet almost everywhere, though a few coasts (notably around Madang and Milne Bay) run on their own calendars.

Where you go changes when you go

01

The Highlands

Cool mountain valleys at 1,500 metres and up, home of the sing-sing festivals. Dry season days are mild, nights genuinely cold.

02

The islands and reefs

Milne Bay, Kimbe Bay and New Ireland: world-class diving year round, with regional wet seasons worth checking.

03

The Sepik and the coast

River culture, carvings and crocodiles. Boats run year round; the drier months are far more comfortable.

Best time to visit Papua New Guinea for

Festivals

August for the Mount Hagen Show, September for Goroka's, the Pacific's greatest gatherings of tribes and dress.

Trekking

June to October for the Kokoda Track and Highlands walks, when the mud is at its most forgiving.

Diving

Year round in Milne Bay and Kimbe Bay; many divers rate the shoulder months either side of the dry season.

Birds of paradise

The dry season, when display trees are active and forest trails are passable at dawn.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Papua New Guinea?
June to October: the dry season around Port Moresby and the Highlands, plus the Mount Hagen and Goroka shows in August and September.
Is the weather the same across Papua New Guinea?
No. Regions run on different calendars, and a few coasts are wettest exactly when Port Moresby is driest, so check your specific stops.
Is Papua New Guinea safe to travel?
It needs planning: use guides or organised tours outside the main centres, arrange airport transfers ahead, and check current advice for Port Moresby and the Highlands.
