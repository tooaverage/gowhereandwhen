Good to know

What is the best time to visit Saudi Arabia?
For Riyadh and AlUla outdoor trips, start with winter. Aseer is a separate summer option.
Is Saudi Arabia too hot in summer?
Riyadh, Jeddah and AlUla have very hot summer days. Aseer highlands differ, so check local conditions.
When should I visit AlUla?
Visit Saudi recommends its cooler winter season for outdoor exploration.
