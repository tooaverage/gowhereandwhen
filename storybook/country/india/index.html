The year at a glance

India is vast and its weather dramatic. Delhi in the north anchors the ratings, where the cool, dry winter from October to March is the obvious window to travel, the pre-monsoon months are intensely hot, and the monsoon brings heavy rain.

The exceptions are worth knowing. The Himalayan north, including Ladakh and the hill stations, is best in summer when the plains bake. The far south and Kerala have their own monsoon timing and stay warm year round, with the southwest rains arriving in June.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Delhi, Rajasthan and the north

October to March is prime, cool and dry, ideal for the Golden Triangle and the desert forts. Summer is brutally hot.

02

The Himalaya and Ladakh

June to September, when the high passes open and the plains are too hot. Winter closes much of the region with snow.

03

Goa, Kerala and the south

November to March for the beaches and backwaters. The southwest monsoon brings rain from June, earlier than the north.

Best time to visit India for

The Golden Triangle

October to March for Delhi, Agra and Jaipur, with comfortable days for sightseeing.

June to September for trekking in Ladakh, Spiti and the mountain valleys.

Tiger safaris

Good to know

What is the best time to visit India?
The cool, dry season from October to March, comfortable across the north and centre. The Himalaya is best in summer and the far south stays warm year round.
When is the monsoon in India?
The southwest monsoon arrives in the south around June and sweeps north, bringing the main rains from July to September across most of the country.
Is summer too hot to visit India?
April to June is extremely hot on the plains and best avoided there, but it is the prime season for the cool Himalayan north.
