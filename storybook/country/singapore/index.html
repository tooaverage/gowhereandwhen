The year at a glance

Sitting almost on the equator, Singapore has no real seasons, just steady heat and humidity with rain possible any day. Singapore is city, country and rating all in one, and the gap between months is small, so any time of year is workable.

The closest thing to a best window is February to April, which tends to be a little drier and brighter, while the northeast monsoon from November to January brings the heaviest, most frequent downpours. Even then the rain usually comes in short, intense bursts, and the city is built to shrug it off.

Where you go changes when you go

01

The city and Marina Bay

Hot and humid year round, with malls, museums and aquariums that shrug off the rain.

02

Gardens and Sentosa

The gardens and the island beaches are best on the brighter, drier days, most common from February to April.

03

A regional hub

A natural launch point for nearby Malaysia, Indonesia and the wider region, each with its own season to consider.

Best time to visit Singapore for

City sightseeing

February to April for the brightest, driest spell, though the indoor sights make any month comfortable.

Gardens and outdoors

The drier months for Gardens by the Bay, the Botanic Gardens and Sentosa without the heaviest rain.

Food and festivals

Year round, with Chinese New Year in late January or February among the liveliest times.

Sales and events

The mid-year Great Singapore Sale and the September Grand Prix weekend draw big crowds.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Singapore?
Any month works, as Singapore is warm and humid year round. February to April is usually the driest, brightest window.
When is the monsoon in Singapore?
The northeast monsoon from November to January brings the wettest, stormiest weather, though rain is possible in any month.
Does it rain every day in Singapore?
Rain is frequent but usually short and intense, and the city is built around it, so it rarely disrupts a trip for long.
