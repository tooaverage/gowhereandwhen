The year at a glance

Turkey bridges Europe and Asia and its climate varies just as much. The month ratings track Istanbul, where spring and autumn bring the most pleasant weather, summer is hot and humid and winter is cool and wet, with the odd snowfall.

The Mediterranean and Aegean coasts run hotter and stay warm later into autumn, while the high interior, including Cappadocia, has cold winters and warm summers. The shoulder seasons suit almost everywhere at once.

Where you go changes when you go

01

Istanbul and the northwest

Best in spring and autumn for the mosques, bazaars and Bosphorus. Summers are hot, winters cool and grey.

02

The Aegean and Mediterranean coast

Long, hot summers and a warm shoulder into October for the beaches and ancient ruins around Bodrum and Antalya.

03

Cappadocia and the interior

Spring and autumn for balloon flights and mild walking weather. Summers are hot and dry, winters cold and snowy.

Best time to visit Turkey for

Istanbul and culture

April to May and September to October for comfortable sightseeing and the Bosphorus at its best.

Cappadocia and balloons

Spring and autumn for the calmest, clearest mornings for hot-air ballooning over the valleys.

Beaches and ruins

June to September on the Turquoise Coast, or the warm shoulder months to dodge the peak heat.

Fewer crowds

November and the early spring bring cooler weather, quiet sites and better value.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Turkey?
Spring, April to May, and autumn, September to October, with warm, comfortable weather and smaller crowds than the hot summer peak.
When is the best time for Cappadocia?
Spring and autumn, for mild walking weather and the calm, clear mornings that suit hot-air balloon flights.
Is the Turkish coast good in summer?
Yes for beaches and warm seas, though July and August are hot and busy. The shoulder months are more comfortable.
