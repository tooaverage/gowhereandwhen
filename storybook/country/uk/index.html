Best time to visit the UK for

Gardens and countryside

May and June, when the gardens bloom, the hedgerows are green and the days are long.

City sightseeing

April to June and September, with comfortable temperatures and lighter crowds than high summer.

Festivals and warmth

July and August for the warmest weather and the busy festival calendar, from Edinburgh to the seaside.

Winter cities

December to February for indoor sights and festive events. Expect short days.

What is on, and when

Festive lights, December

Cities glow with Christmas lights and markets despite the short, grey days.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit the UK?
Summer brings the warmest days. Compare your chosen city by month, since rain and temperature differ.
Does it rain all the time in the UK?
No. Rain varies by place and month, with no rain-free season.
When is the warmest month in London?
July has the highest average daytime high at Heathrow, about 24°C.
