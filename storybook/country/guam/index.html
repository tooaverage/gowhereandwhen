The year at a glance

Guam is America in the western Pacific: a US territory with big resort beaches on Tumon Bay, Chamorro villages and fiestas in the south, and a dense layer of WWII history. It is warm year round, split between a dry trade-wind season and a wet, stormy one. We rate on Tumon, the resort strip.

January to April is the pick: rainfall drops as low as 55 mm in March, humidity eases and the reef flats stay calm. From July the rain builds fast, peaking August to October at 300 to 350 mm a month, exactly when typhoon risk is highest. Hotels are engineered for storms, but that half of the year needs flexible plans.

Best time to visit Guam for

Beaches and resorts

January to April, for the surest sun on Tumon Bay's protected lagoon.

Diving and snorkelling

The dry season for calm, clear water, including the Blue Hole and double-war wrecks of Apra Harbor.

Village fiestas

Year round: southern villages rotate patron-saint fiestas, and visitors are genuinely welcome.

Best value

The wet season, with resort deals balancing the daily showers and storm-watch caveats.

Good to know

What is the best time to visit Guam?
January to April, the dry season, with February and March the driest months of all.
When is typhoon season in Guam?
Roughly June to December, peaking August to October. Guam takes direct hits every few years, and its hotels are built to ride them out.
Do I need a US visa for Guam?
Guam follows US entry rules, with a Guam-CNMI visa waiver for several Asian countries on short visits. US dollars, US plugs, US phone networks.
